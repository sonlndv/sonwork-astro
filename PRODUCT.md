# PRODUCT.md — sonwork.org

## Register
**Brand** (design IS the product). A personal **project showcase** + portfolio. The visitor's
impression is the deliverable — it has to feel made, not generated.

## What it is
sonwork.org — Sơn Lê's personal site. Primary job: **showcase the projects he builds** as a
solo founder running a one-person holding company (Perfeat, Paddock, the agent fleet/brain, etc.).
Secondary: an **About** (who he is) and a **field-log / writing** stream. Showcase first, blog second.

## Users
- **Other builders & founders** — peers who'll recognize the craft and the one-person-holdco play.
- **Potential collaborators / clients** — people deciding whether to work with him.
- **The curious** — anyone who lands and should leave thinking "who is this and how does he ship this much?"

## Desired outcome
A visitor leaves impressed by the *volume and quality of what he ships*, and remembers the site
itself. Memorable, high-craft, fast. The work is the proof; the design carries the energy.

## Brand personality (three words)
**Fast. Engineered. Bold.** A builder with race-team precision and a driver's confidence. Energy
without noise; everything is deliberate and quick.

## Aesthetic lane (name the reference)
**F1 race-team telemetry meets a driver's personal brand** — think the pit-wall/HUD precision of a
race team fused with the bold, charismatic stance of a driver's site (landonorris.com). Dark canvas,
one charged electric-blue accent (Red Bull blue), technical type, motion that feels quick.

## Anti-references (do NOT become)
- Lime-green (Lando's actual color) — we use electric blue instead.
- Warm-paper / editorial / "ink on paper" (the abandoned direction).
- Generic SaaS-cream, corporate-navy template, or overly-minimal "safe" portfolio.
- Sci-fi cliché (Orbitron/neon-grid). Technical, not costume.

## Strategic design principles
1. **Show the work, big.** Projects are the hero content — presented with scale and confidence, not buried in a list.
2. **Telemetry precision.** Structure, alignment, mono data-labels, real numbers — it should read like an instrument, not a brochure.
3. **Charged, not loud.** One electric-blue accent used sharply against dark; restraint makes it hit.
4. **Quick.** Fast to load, fast motion (ease-out), nothing that makes you wait.
5. **Distinctive.** If it could be any builder's template, it failed. A clear POV.
