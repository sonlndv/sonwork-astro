# Writing & Web

Everything with Son's name on it that a stranger can read: the site, public writing, the
Arx brand surface.

**Publishing is approval-gated.** `~/sonwork-astro` auto-deploys to sonwork.org on push to
`main` — pushing IS publishing and needs Son's explicit approval, every time.

## Scope

- `~/sonwork-astro` — sonwork.org. Astro site, posts, reports. Primary. Auto-deploys.
- `~/arx-web` — Arx marketing site.
- `~/arx-brand` — brand system, design spec, operating-structure pages.
- `~/projects/personal/public` — drafts in progress. Shared with the Personal project.

Drafting happens in `personal/public/`. Moving a piece into `sonwork-astro` is the decision
to publish it, and that decision is Son's.

## Voice

Public writing uses **Son's voice, not Jarvis's**. Write like a real builder with taste,
scars, and a point of view: sharp, honest, specific, grounded.

Avoid fake excitement, generic thought-leadership, academic padding, corporate euphemism,
and canned openings. Skills: `humanizer`, `grounded-citations` for anything with numbers.

## The rule that protects Son

**Never trade privacy for authenticity.** Do not name a client, an employer, a customer, or
internal Arx strategy to make a piece feel real. The specific detail that makes writing good
must come from Son's own experience, not from confidential context.

If a draft needs a real example and the only one available is confidential, say so and ask —
do not invent one and do not use it.

## Conventions

- Every factual or numeric claim carries a source. Skill: `grounded-citations`.
- Marketing pages sell rather than explain. Skill: `marketing-website-build`.
- Design changes go through the brand system in `~/arx-brand`, not ad-hoc styling.
- Preview locally before proposing a publish.

## Boundaries

- No push to `main` on `sonwork-astro` without explicit approval in that session.
- No external announcement, no social post, no outreach without approval.
- Arx client identities never appear. Dynamic Education is never named publicly.
- Drafts mentioning private employment or family stay in `personal/`, not here.
