The public home was a full-viewport opening with a badge, a pulse figure,
and a stats row. That made the archive feel like the identity. Sơn asked
for a new story and a new composition, then for the rest of the UI.

The opening is a short identity frame. Copy is humble: operator, Vietnam,
trying to build, Lever is the core. Nav is Projects, Readings, About.
Readings keep their URLs; the index and each reading now share the same
intro and a dek under the title. Booking stays hidden. web.sonwork.org
was not touched.
