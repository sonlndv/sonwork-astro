Sơn decided the second project today and recorded it in the brain earlier in
the day; this afternoon he asked for it on the site. Lever is an operations
practice enabled by AI: a process a business already runs by hand, learned
from the people who run it, rebuilt to take less of their time. Sonar senses,
Lever moves. It enters as `building`, order 02, the first project to come in
through PROJECTS.md since that file was written.

What is true on day one: one engagement is under way, a payroll run for a
small education business, and its pay rules are still being confirmed with
the client, so the system produces plausible output rather than correct output
until they are. The client is not named here and will not be. The practice's
public name is not decided; Lever is the working name and the site says so.
No mark until Sơn locks one. The home's portfolio card carries Lever's latest
thing automatically, which today is this entry.
