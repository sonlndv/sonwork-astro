## What it is

Lever is the second project. Sonar senses; Lever moves. It is an operations
practice: I take a process a business already runs by hand every week, learn
its rules from the people who run it, and rebuild it so it takes less of their
time and makes fewer mistakes. AI is the enabler, not the product. Nobody is
sold a model. They are sold a process that costs less to run than it did. The
name is the thesis: a small input at the right point, a large output. Lever is
what I call it; the practice's public name is not decided.

## How it runs

One engagement at a time, always from an existing process, never a blank page.
The rules belong to the client and are confirmed in their own words before
anything runs on them. Anything that touches money, staff or customers stops
for a person to approve it. Each client gets an isolated setup that cannot see
another client's data, or mine. Every correction the client makes is kept, so
the same correction is not needed twice. The first engagement is under way: a
payroll run for a small education business, from recorded hours to a payslip
the owner reviews and releases, with the pay rules still being confirmed with
the client. Sonar does the research when an engagement needs it. I decide; the
stack builds and runs.

## Where it goes

The test is whether the client's process got better: less time, fewer errors,
less dependence on one person's memory. The first client's numbers are not in
yet, so nothing here claims a result. A second client is not decided, and the
public name is not decided. Both are my call.
