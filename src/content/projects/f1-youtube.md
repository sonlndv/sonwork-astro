## What it is

A Vietnamese-language, data-led F1 channel. Verification is the founding rule.
It gets built the same way as the research engine: the team finds, researches
and prepares; I decide what ships.

## Why it is second

The research engine has to work properly first. F1 is the simpler theme to
prove the next step on: from research to a script to a video.

## The endgame

Not a content engine and not a research engine alone, but a full YouTube
engine, from finding a topic to a published video, run by the team.
