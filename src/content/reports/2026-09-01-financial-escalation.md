Financial escalation is the part of a conflict that shows up in public data first.
Reserve freezes, settlement bans and correspondent-banking withdrawals are visible
weeks or months before any movement a defence desk would log.

## What this implies for small open economies

Small economies rarely pick a side before the ladder is well advanced. They become
collateral through payment rails they do not control, which is a different exposure
from trade exposure and is usually measured badly.
