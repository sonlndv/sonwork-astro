I'm Son, a business guy in Vietnam. I don't have a software engineering background. I'm figuring out what I can build with AI, learning as I go, and writing down what works and what doesn't. I'm curious how far this can go as AI gets smarter.

What I build is mostly things I want to use myself: a hub for the daily games I play, a keyboard plugin for my desktop, tools for my own writing setup. The work is on the [Work](/work) page. What I learn from it goes on the [Writing](/writing) page, failures included.

Two small tools for that writing setup:

- [Omarchy theme for Obsidian](https://github.com/sonlndv/obsidian-omarchy-theme): an Obsidian theme built from Omarchy's colour palettes, light and dark, with IBM Plex Mono.
- [Omawrite notes vault](https://github.com/sonlndv/omawrite): my fork of Omarchy's writing app, with a notes folder, `[[wikilinks]]`, a graph view and a quick switcher.
