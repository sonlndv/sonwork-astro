OpenMaus is an agent workspace for building and managing your own team of AI employees, using supported AI subscriptions you already have. [Supported connections](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/blob/main/README.md#quick-start).

If you know Claude, ChatGPT or Meta Muse, the idea will feel familiar. OpenMaus gives you a workspace for your AI team, with a choice of supported providers rather than tying the whole team to one. Its core is also [open source under Apache 2.0](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/blob/main/LICENSE): you can modify and repackage the code under the license terms; the enterprise folder has a separate license. Yes, you can give your fork a dramatic new name, too.

Give them jobs: a researcher to gather information, a writer to prepare drafts, a planner to organise work. Each bot can have its own role and instructions. You decide how it should work and connect the tools it needs. [Bot setup](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/blob/main/docs/presets.md).

Work with each bot directly, or set up a Chief of Staff to coordinate the team, delegate tasks and bring back reports. You set the direction and review their work. [Team handoffs](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/blob/main/docs/runtime-contract.md).

For a first task, you could give a bot an article and ask: “Explain the main ideas in plain language and flag anything unclear.” Read its answer alongside the original and ask for changes where needed.

Available engines, models and tools depend on your account and setup. [Connection details](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/blob/main/docs/custom-engines.md).

Start with the [official download](https://www.openmausbot.com/download), connect a supported AI engine or account, and create your first bot. Give it one small job and review the result before building out your team.
